NOVATECH COMPANY INFORMATION

NovaTech is a fictional technology company founded in 2021. The company develops software, consumer electronics, and cloud-based services for students, professionals, and small businesses.

The company headquarters is located in Lagos, Nigeria. NovaTech currently has approximately 120 employees working across software engineering, product design, marketing, customer support, and administration.

NovaTech's mission is to make useful technology more accessible and affordable for everyday users.

The company operates from Monday to Friday. Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, although some teams may have flexible schedules depending on their responsibilities.

NovaTech's main departments are Software Engineering, Product Design, Marketing, Customer Support, Sales, Finance, and Human Resources.

The company provides its employees with access to internal training programs, technical workshops, and professional development resources.

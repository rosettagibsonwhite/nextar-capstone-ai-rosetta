NOVATECH EMPLOYEE POLICIES

NovaTech employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees should submit leave requests through the company's internal employee portal at least five working days before the requested start date.

Employees who are sick should notify their team leader as soon as possible and provide appropriate documentation when required.

NovaTech uses a hybrid working policy. Employees may work remotely for up to three days per week, depending on their role and team requirements. Some positions may require employees to work from the office more frequently.

Employees are expected to maintain professional communication with colleagues and customers. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and intentional misuse of company resources are prohibited.

All employees must protect confidential company information. Internal documents, customer information, passwords, and unreleased product information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals.

NovaTech provides employees with a one-hour lunch break during the standard working day. Employees may arrange the timing of their lunch break with their team when their responsibilities allow.

Employees are encouraged to report technical problems, security concerns, or workplace issues to their team leader or the appropriate department.
